# 📊 Adecco HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **HR Analytics and Employee Attrition Analysis** at Adecco India.

Adecco India is experiencing an increase in employee turnover, particularly among **junior-level employees in the Sales department**. High employee attrition can increase recruitment and training costs, reduce productivity, and affect team performance.

The objective of this project is to use **Excel-based data analysis** to identify the factors associated with employee attrition, job satisfaction, employee performance, and retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Adecco India wants to understand:

* Why employees are leaving the company
* Which departments have higher attrition
* How job satisfaction affects employee retention
* Whether salary, overtime, tenure, job level, and other factors influence attrition
* How HR can use data to improve employee satisfaction and retention

The analysis can help HR and management make **data-driven decisions** to improve employee retention and reduce turnover.

4. Job satisfaction across different job roles

## 📈 Key HR Metrics

The analysis focuses on important HR metrics such as:

### Attrition Rate

Percentage of employees who leave the company during a given period.

### Job Satisfaction Score

Average employee satisfaction score based on job satisfaction ratings.

### Engagement / Involvement

Measures employee involvement and engagement with their work.

### Performance Rating

Average employee performance rating.

### Employee Tenure

Average length of time employees have worked at the company.

## 🔍 Key Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Identify patterns in employee attrition
* Find departments and roles with higher employee turnover
* Understand the relationship between salary and attrition
* Analyse the impact of overtime on employee retention
* Study the effect of job satisfaction and work-life balance
* Examine the relationship between employee tenure and attrition

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, HR teams can consider:

* Focusing retention efforts on high-attrition departments and job roles
* Reviewing employee workload and overtime practices
* Improving job satisfaction and work-life balance
* Providing career growth and promotion opportunities
* Strengthening employee training and development programs
* Reviewing compensation for roles with higher attrition
* Monitoring junior-level employees who may be more likely to leave
* Using employee data regularly to identify early signs of attrition
